about=CRESTA evaluates climate change resilience by comparing the bioclimatic niche (Bio1–Bio19) of a species' native distribution against a target area. The 5-layer ML ensemble (PCA, GMM, Isolation Forest, One-Class SVM, Mahalanobis Chi², K-Means) produces a composite score with climate (80%) and topography (20%) weighting. Score classes: A ≥ 80 Very High · B ≥ 65 High · C ≥ 50 Moderate · D ≥ 35 Low · E < 35 Very Low. Optional variables: solar radiation, wind speed, vapour pressure. Compatible with QGIS 3.16+ and QGIS 4.x (Qt5/Qt6). Developed at Süleyman Demirel University with Claude AI (Anthropic).
